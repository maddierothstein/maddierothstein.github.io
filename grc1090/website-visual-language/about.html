Maddie's Sugar House is a locally owned bakery in San Luis Obispo, California, dedicated to creating delicious, freshly baked treats for every occasion. From beautifully decorated cakes and flavorful cupcakes to classic cookies and other sweet treats, we offer a variety of desserts made to bring a little extra sweetness to everyday moments.

Our bakery focuses on combining quality ingredients, delicious flavors, and thoughtful presentation to create treats that are just as enjoyable to look at as they are to eat. Whether you're stopping by for a sweet afternoon pick-me-up, picking up dessert to share with family and friends, or ordering a cake for a birthday, celebration, or special event, Maddie's Sugar House has something for everyone.

We believe that desserts are more than just something sweet; they're a way to celebrate life's little moments, bring people together, and create lasting memories. That's why we strive to provide a welcoming atmosphere where every customer feels at home and can find something they'll love.

At Maddie's Sugar House, our goal is simple: to make every day a little sweeter, one treat at a time. We look forward to being a part of your everyday celebrations and special occasions!
