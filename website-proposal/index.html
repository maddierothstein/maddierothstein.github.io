Introduction

Maddie's Sugar House

My company is called Maddie's Sugar House, a local bakery in SLO that sells fresh cakes, cupcakes, cookies, and other sweet treats. The bakery focuses on making delicious desserts for everyday treats and special occasions. Maddie's Sugar House is a welcoming place where customers can find something sweet to enjoy or share with family and friends.

Target audience

The target audience for Maddie's Sugar House is anyone and everyone who enjoys desserts. Customers might visit the website to buy a sweet treat for themselves or to order something for a birthday, anniversary, graduation, holiday, party, or other celebration.

The main goals are to be able to look at the menu, easily find something you like, check prices, and place an order. The website should make it easy for customers to find what they need while also giving them ideas for diffrent occasions.
